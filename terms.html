Lumora+ subscription

  • Lumora+ Monthly is an auto-renewable subscription. It includes unlimited hints, double shards from levels, a daily shard bonus and exclusive cosmetics while it is active.
  • New subscribers may be offered a free trial. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts and you are charged the monthly price shown in the app.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically each month unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your device's Settings under your Apple ID › Subscriptions.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

Shards and other purchases

Shards are virtual items for use in Lumora only. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. Refunds are handled by Apple according to its policies; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Non-consumable purchases (such as the Starter Pack and Unlock All Chapters) can be restored on your devices with Restore Purchases in the shop.
