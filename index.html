Lumora: Light Puzzles

Bend the light. Rotate mirrors, prisms and portals to guide glowing beams into crystals.

Frequently asked questions

Does Lumora cost anything?
No. Lumora is free: no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no account. Shards are earned by playing and can't be bought.

Does my progress sync between devices?
Not yet. Progress is saved on each device.

I can't tell some colours apart.
Turn on Colour symbols in Settings. Every coloured piece then shows red, green and blue dots: a dot is filled in its own colour when the piece's colour contains it, so mixed colours can be read without relying on hue. High contrast makes beams and outlines bolder.

Is there a leaderboard?
Yes. The Daily puzzle (fewest taps) and endless runs (longest run) have Game Center leaderboards, and there are Game Center achievements to earn. Sign in to Game Center in Settings to take part.
