Lumora+ subscription

  • Lumora+ is an auto-renewable subscription, offered monthly or yearly. While it is active it opens every chapter (1–11) and includes unlimited hints, double shards from levels, a daily shard bonus, unlimited endless runs and the Lumora+ and full-game cosmetics. When it ends, chapters and cosmetics that you got only through Lumora+ lock again; chapters you opened with stars or shards, or with Unlock Every Chapter, stay open, and your progress and stars are kept. The price and length of each plan are shown in the app before you subscribe.
  • New subscribers may be offered a free trial (3 days on the monthly plan, 1 week on the yearly plan). If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts and you are charged the price shown in the app.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your device's Settings under your Apple Account › Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
  • Your first Lumora+ subscription (including its free trial) comes with a one-time gift of 250 shards. The gift is not repeated if you subscribe again and is not refundable.

Unlock Every Chapter and shards

Chapters 1–3 are free. Unlock Every Chapter is a one-time purchase that opens chapters 4–11 and the Solar Flare, Dusk and Synthwave cosmetics for good, with or without Lumora+, and comes with a one-time gift of 250 shards. Lumora+ opens the same chapters only while it is active; chapters can also be opened one at a time with shards earned by playing. Shards are earned only by playing and are virtual items for use in Lumora only. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

Refunds are handled by Apple according to its policies; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. One-time purchases and subscriptions can be restored on your devices with Restore purchases in the shop, the locked-chapter screen or Settings.
