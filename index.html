Frequently asked questions

I bought something but don't see it.
Open the shop and tap Restore Purchases. Shard packs are credited as soon as Apple confirms the purchase; if a purchase is still "pending" (for example waiting for Ask to Buy approval), it will arrive once approved.

How do I cancel Lumora+?
On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Lumora+ and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep the benefits until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does my progress sync between devices?
Not yet. Progress is saved on each device. Purchases (Starter Pack, Unlock All Chapters and Lumora+) follow your Apple ID and can be restored on any device.

I can't tell some colours apart.
Turn on Colour symbols in Settings. Every coloured piece then shows red, green and blue dots, so mixed colours can be read without relying on hue. High contrast makes beams and outlines bolder.

Is there a leaderboard?
Yes. The Daily Challenge (fewest taps) and Endless mode (furthest puzzle) have Game Center leaderboards. Sign in to Game Center in Settings to take part.
