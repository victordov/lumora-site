Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This policy explains how Lumora: Light Puzzles ("Lumora", "the app"), developed by Victor Dovgaliuc ("we", "us"), handles information. The short version: we don't collect it.

In short
  • We do not collect, store or receive any personal data.
  • The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and uses no third-party SDKs.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • Purchases and Game Center are handled entirely by Apple.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game, Lumora saves the following on your device only: levels completed and stars earned, your shard balance, unlocked chapters and cosmetics, settings (sound, haptics, accessibility, reminders), daily reward and quest progress, and the identifiers of purchases already credited (so a purchase is never applied twice). This data never leaves your device through Lumora. We have no servers and cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app. Depending on your device settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which are governed by Apple.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple ID, payment card or billing details. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase succeeded, which it verifies on your device to unlock what you bought. You can manage or cancel a Lumora+ subscription in your device's Settings under your Apple ID › Subscriptions. Apple's handling of this information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center and are optional. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores for the Daily Challenge (number of taps) and Endless mode (furthest puzzle reached) are sent to Apple and shown to other players together with your Game Center nickname, according to your Game Center settings. We do not receive your Game Center account information. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device's Settings.

Notifications

If you choose to turn on reminders, Lumora schedules local notifications on your device (for example, when a new Daily Challenge is ready). They are created on the device itself; no push server is involved and no data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in your device's Settings.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share after solving a level, the app creates an image of the board and your score and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it.

Children

Lumora is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

Your choices and rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. To remove everything Lumora has stored, delete the app from your device. Game Center data can be managed through Apple.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example if a future version adds a feature that requires collecting data, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please open an issue at github.com/victordov/lumora-site/issues.
